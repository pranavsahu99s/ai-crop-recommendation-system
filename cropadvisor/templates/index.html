How it works

  1. Add your plot

    Location, land area and the season you plan to sow in.

  2. Enter soil values

    N, P, K and pH from your soil health card, or upload the report file.

  3. Weather is fetched

    Temperature, rainfall and humidity for your place over that season.

  4. Get ranked crops

    The top three crops with a confidence score. Download the report as a PDF.

Based on your own data

Recommendations use your soil test and the weather recorded at your location, not national averages.

Honest about uncertainty

If your conditions do not clearly suit any crop, the result is marked low-confidence instead of guessed.

Kept for later

Every recommendation is saved to your history with the inputs and weather that produced it.
